# Code of conduct

Privacy Launcher should be a place where anyone can report a bug, ask a
question or propose a change without being harassed or made to feel
unwelcome, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not native English speakers,
  and many are new to Android or to open source.
- Assume good faith. Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- Respect privacy, in keeping with what this project is for. Do not post
  anyone's personal information, and check your own screenshots and logs
  for it before you share them.
- Stay on topic, and accept a maintainer's decision about what fits the
  project, even when you disagree with it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexual language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or continuing after being asked to
  stop.

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and
anywhere someone represents the project.

## How to report a problem

Report it privately through GitHub: this repository's **Security** tab,
then **Report a vulnerability**, saying that it is about conduct. Only the
maintainers can see it. If the problem is with a maintainer, use GitHub's
own **Report abuse** link on their profile or comment instead.

Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and code, give a
warning, or ban someone from the project for a time or for good. The
response will fit what happened, and the person reported will be told what
was decided, without revealing who reported them.
